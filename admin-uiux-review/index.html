For sign-off · Settings, People and Reports

Settings, People and Reports: one look

A design reviewer who did not build these screens went through every Settings, People and Reports screen at laptop width (1366) and phone width (375). Each screen was judged against one pattern, taken from the pathway screens, because the standard is that it all looks the same and is consistent.

What we found

There are 31 findings: 8 broken, 14 where a screen looks different from the pattern, 6 about words, and 3 about keyboard, contrast and touch. 12 would be noticed by UM6P's admin on Monday. One is already fixed in an open pull request and two are partly fixed. Nothing here repeats what you signed off yesterday; where a finding extends one, it says so.

Broken things come first. Each finding shows the screen now beside the pathway part it should match, and the change in one sentence. The screens are the local review copy with UM6P demonstration data. The reviewer did not check a real phone, Safari, a screen reader by ear, the dark look or the live site.

Screen 1 of 6 · First

The one pattern

Every finding below is judged against this one pattern, taken from the pathway screens. Skills could not be used as the reference: its phone screen was an error page on the review copy.

  • Header band: brand red across the work area, white title, one plain line under it, the pill and the page's own actions inside the band.
  • Tabs: plain words, a red underline on the one you are on, the same distance below the band on every page.
  • Toolbar: filters on the left, then search, then one main button at the right end.
  • Rows: one card, rows divided by a line, the name on the left, a status pill, the whole row is the tap. On a phone the pill drops under the name.
  • Switches: an On/Off switch with the word beside it.
  • Buttons: main is brand red, 44 to 48px tall, white text; secondary is white with a grey line; quiet is a text link.
  • Empty states: one sentence that says what is missing and what to do, where the content would be.
  • Words: "and", never "&"; sentence case; no tracking terms.
  • Phone: the bottom bar, and "Reading only." straight under the band, before anything else.

Screen 2 of 6 · First

What is broken

Eight things do not work, or work against the person using them. Six of them would be noticed by UM6P on Monday.

  1. U1Reports, Entrepreneurial Mindset Profile, both widthsBefore Monday

    The page never opens. It says "I could not load the Entrepreneurial Mindset Profile". Its one read takes longer than the admin waits, so the admin gives up every time.

    Change: Make the profile report open, by having it answer within the time the admin waits.

  2. U2Reports, Everything that happened, both widthsBefore Monday

    The list is the admin's own error records: "Demo Admin error uncaught Admin breadcrumb: error.uncaught", with the code again on the right in grey type. 24 of the first 25 rows are errors.

    Change: Show only what learners and admins did, in words, and leave the system's own error records out.

  3. U3Reports, Your organisation, 1366 and 375Before Monday

    "What learners did each day" spikes to 6,394 on 21 Oct, and the label says "statements". At a glance says it left out 6,393 records it could not match. The chart likely draws the records every other figure leaves out. Inference, not proven: the builder should confirm. Already in hand: Zavmo-v2 #3663 changes the left-out sentence only, not the chart. Extends R8.

    Change: Count the same learning in this chart as in the figures above it, and call it "things learners did".

  4. U4Reports, Tutor usage, both widthsBefore Monday

    "By learner" is a heading with nothing under it. Below it, twelve months of zeros in a table.

    Change: Under "By learner", say "Nobody used the tutor this month", and show one line instead of twelve empty months.

  5. U5Reports, Criterion by criterion, 1366Before Monday

    The pathway picker is 240px wide for a 377px name. It shows "Entrepreneurial and Innovation" and the arrow sits on the last letter.

    Change: Make the pathway picker wide enough to show the whole pathway name.

  6. U6Settings, History, audit log, 1366After Monday

    The From and To boxes cut the date: "dd/mm/yy" with the year half gone.

    Change: Widen the From and To date boxes so the whole date shows.

  7. U7People, 375Before Monday

    The search hint is cut: "Find by name, email, job or departmer".

    Change: Shorten the People search hint to "Find a person" so it fits a phone.

  8. U31Every page, at a normal laptop windowBefore Monday

    At a 900px-high window, a 60px empty dark strip sits above the red band on every page. On a pathway at 1366 it hides the "Learning pathways" back link. Full-page screenshots hide it, so it is easy to miss.

    Change: Remove the empty strip, so the band starts at the top of the work area and the back link shows.

Screen 3 of 6 · Look the same

Look the same as pathways

Her standard: it all needs to look the same and consistent. Each of these differs from the one pattern the pathway screens set, however small.

  1. U8People and Groups, 1366After Monday

    Two reds and three sizes for the main button. "Add people" and "New group" are a darker red, 13px text, 44px and 36px tall. "New pathway" and Settings' "Add to team" are brand red, 44 to 48px, 15px.

    Change: Make every main button the pathway's red, at one height and one text size.

  2. U9Groups, 1366After Monday

    The toolbar is the wrong way round: "New group" on the left, search on the right. People and Pathways put search first and the button at the right end.

    Change: Put Groups' search on the left and "New group" at the right end.

  3. U10Settings, 1366After Monday

    The tabs sit 20px lower than on Pathways and People. The underline is at 202px against 182px.

    Change: Set Settings' tabs at the same distance below the band as Pathways and People.

  4. U11Reports pages, both widthsAfter Monday

    "Export" sits alone on its own row above the filters, 76px of height for one button. On Pathways the action sits at the end of the filter row.

    Change: Put Export at the right end of the filter row on every report.

  5. U12Reports filters, 1366After Monday

    Period, Who and Learning pathway are plain dropdowns with the label above. People's filters are rounded boxes with the label inside. Pathways uses chips.

    Change: Draw the report filters in the same boxes as People's filters.

  6. U13Report pages, both widthsAfter Monday

    Five reports have no line under the title: At a glance, Your organisation, Goals, Growth mindset signals, the profile report. The others have one. The band is 128px on some and 160 to 184px on others.

    Change: Give every report one plain line under its title, saying what it answers.

  7. U14A group's page, both widthsAfter Monday

    "5 people · Made 21 October 2026" and the description sit under the band in grey. The pathway puts its line inside the band.

    Change: Move the group's line into the band, under its name.

  8. U15Your profile, both widthsAfter Monday

    "Save changes" is on the left and "Reset" on the right. Settings has them the other way round.

    Change: Put Reset left and Save changes right on Your profile, as in Settings.

  9. U16Every page, 375Before Monday

    "Reading only." moves about and changes its words. Settings: above the tabs. People: under the search. A person: on top of the preview box with no gap. The pathway: straight under the band. Four wordings: "open this on a computer", "use a computer", "open it on a computer", "Export works here too".

    Change: Put the "Reading only." line straight under the band on every page, ending "open it on a computer".

  10. U17Your profile, 375 and 1366After Monday

    On a phone it is a full form with Save, and no "Reading only." line, unlike every other page. The top bar has no page name at either width.

    Change: Name the top bar "Your profile", and show it as reading only on a phone like the rest.

  11. U18Settings, Zavmo & data, 1366After Monday

    The five "Which changes it may propose" items stay as tick boxes under "What I may do". The heading says "it" where the card says "I". Already in hand: #874 turns the top two into switches and keeps these five as ticks on purpose. Extends S17.

    Change: Make the five change types On/Off switches too, under "Which changes I may propose".

  12. U19Settings, Zavmo & data, 1366After Monday

    The content library part breaks the page's pattern. "Content libraries" is a bare heading outside any card. The connect card has smaller text and different inner spacing from the cards above. It asks for "API base URL", "API key" and "SCORM packages".

    Change: Put the content library in one card like the others, and ask for "Library address" and "Access key".

  13. U20Settings, Team & roles, 375After Monday

    "What each role can do" keeps two columns. The role name sits in a wide empty column, and each description wraps to five or six lines.

    Change: On a phone, put each role's description under its name.

  14. U21Settings tabs and Zavmo & data, both widthsAfter Monday

    "Team & roles", "Security & SSO", "Zavmo & data", "Cohorts & membership", "Learner tags & flags". The pathway writes "See and do".

    Change: Write "and" for "&" in Settings, and call the tab "Security and sign-in".

Screen 4 of 6 · Words

Words

Places where the words are technical, inconsistent or about something that has gone.

  1. U22Reports, both widthsAfter Monday

    Three names for one action: "Export", "Export CSV", "Download CSV".

    Change: Call it "Export" on every report.

  2. U23Reports library and At a glance, 1366Before Monday

    One period written three ways on two screens. "17 October 2026, 09:00 to 24 October 2026, 09:00 UTC"; "17 to 23 October 2026, UTC"; "17 October 2026, 09:01 to 24 October 2026, 09:01 UTC".

    Change: Write a report's period one way everywhere: "17 to 23 October".

  3. U24Reports, Tutor usage, both widthsBefore Monday

    "Tokens 0 in · 0 out" and the Tokens columns are a technical measure a client cannot act on.

    Change: Remove Tokens from the client's Tutor usage and keep Credits.

  4. U25Reports, Outcomes, both widthsBefore Monday

    "at the outcome's Bloom level", and each outcome led by a code ("LO1").

    Change: Say "at the level the outcome asks for", and drop the LO codes.

  5. U26Reports, Feedback questions, bandAfter Monday

    "Each rate is per time the question was shown."

    Change: Say "A rate is answers out of times the question was shown."

  6. U27Settings, Organisation, conversation, 1366Before Monday

    Zavmo opens with "I have no contact email for your organisation", for a field the fixes remove. Already in hand: **covered by #866** (the opener now names a missing time zone only).

    Change: Open on what is still missing, not on a removed field.

Screen 5 of 6 · Keyboard and access

Keyboard, contrast and touch

Keyboard order, one contrast failure, and one touch target too small for a finger. Screen-reader names all passed: no unnamed control on any of the 26 screens.

  1. U28Every page, 1366After Monday

    From a fresh page, the "Skip to" link is the 12th Tab stop, after the rail and the conversation. Settings has no skip link: its first tab is the 12th stop. On People, Tab reaches the four filters before the search box above them.

    Change: Make "Skip to the page" the first Tab stop everywhere, Settings included.

  2. U29Every inner page's back link, both widthsBefore Monday

    The arrow before "Reports", "People" or "Groups" is grey on red, about 1.4:1. The words are white and pass.

    Change: Draw the back arrow white, like its words.

  3. U30Reports, Your organisation and the pathway report, 375After Monday

    The figures in the bars ("19", "15", "9") are links 12 to 20px wide and 26px tall.

    Change: Make the whole bar the tap, not the number.
