People: what an admin sees, and what we recommend

People is where a client's admin adds their people, puts them in groups, sets who manages whom, and gives them pathways. This page walks every People screen, shows where each value comes from, and recommends what to change. Each recommendation has a number, so you can sign them off one at a time.

What we found

People is in better shape than Settings. Almost everything on it is the client's own records, which is where it belongs, and the two things SLM decides are respected: whether a manager may read what a learner wrote, and whether a pathway's own criteria show on a person. The weakness is seats. SLM holds how many seats a client has bought, and nothing counts them: an admin can add or import as many people as they like. The rest is small: one out-of-date sentence on every group page, and a due date that lives in two places.

Screens are from um6p-sandbox.admin.zavmo.ai, the UM6P demonstration client, with invented people. Each is shown at laptop width (1366) and phone width (375). Recommendations marked Before Monday are the ones UM6P's admin would notice; the rest can follow.

Screen 1 of 6 · Everyone

Everyone at the client

The list of everyone at the client: search, filter by status, job, department, location or level, and sort. Add a person, or import many from a spreadsheet. The filters are built from the client's own records, so each client sees its own jobs and departments.

Seats are the gap. How many seats a client has is a client detail, held in SLM. Adding or importing people never checks it, so a client can go past what they bought without anyone knowing.

What we recommend

  1. P1Count seats when people are added or imported. Show the count on People (seats used out of seats bought), warn as it gets close, and stop at the limit with a line saying who to ask. The limit stays in SLM. #2933After Monday

Screen 2 of 6 · Everyone

One person

One person's page: their details and tags, the pathways they have, their learning record, Future Fluency, their pathway's own criteria, where they started, and what their manager has seen at work.

Two parts follow SLM, and both work. The learning record shows what the learner wrote only where SLM lets managers see learning detail. The pathway's own criteria show only where SLM has "Evidence on the client's criteria" on. On um6p-sandbox the page also carries a "Not built yet" banner for sharing with managers and console access; S10 on the Settings page takes those banners off for UM6P.

Screen 3 of 6 · Groups and managers

Groups

Groups are how a client gives a pathway to many people at once. A group can be standing (new members get the pathway too) or a snapshot (only today's members). A give can have a due date and an end date. All the client's own, which is right.

Every client's group page says gives made before 13 September reached nobody. Before that date a give did not enrol anyone; it was fixed then. The sentence is wrong for any client that started after it, UM6P included. The due date is also set here, on each give, while the signed pathway walkthrough sets "Due by" on the pathway's Launch chapter.

What we recommend

  1. P2Take the 13 September sentence off, except on a group that really has a give from before that date. A#778Before Monday
  2. P3Set the due date once, on the pathway's Launch. A give to a group starts from that date and can change it for that group only. #3522After Monday
