10Reasoning scenarios

A couple of short scenarios to understand how you reason.

These aren't tests with right answers — I'm interested in how you think through trade-offs, not whether you pick the obvious option. Type as much or as little as feels natural.

Your operation has two paths to reduce resource intensity. Path 1 delivers a 15% reduction quickly but locks you into a single supplier. Path 2 delivers 8% now with optionality to scale to 25% over two years, but needs cross-team alignment. How would you reason through the choice, and what would you want to know first?

A colleague says: "Sustainability is a cost, not a capability." How would you respond, using something specific from your own work or context?
