10Your personalised journey

Your One Court Africa journey.

Anchored in the impact-investment domain, sequenced to your starting profile, with experiential moments paced to your engagement comfort.

Six progression stages

1
Understand · foundations of impact investing

Concepts, vocabulary and the African investment landscape. HBS-style cases tied to your impact priorities (youth & sport, founder representation).

2
Interpret · ecosystem analysis

Read patterns across founders, ventures and capital flows. Lagos, Nairobi, Cape Town, Cairo, Casablanca case explorations.

3
Apply · venture analysis frameworks

Trade-off tools, due-diligence shortcuts for non-finance backgrounds, founder/team assessment. Hands-on with two live ventures.

4
Engage · founders & coaching circles

Live conversations with African founders. Coaching circles paced to your engagement comfort — supported first, then progressively independent.

5
Evaluate · investment simulations

Run-throughs of pitch evaluation, capital allocation under uncertainty, and judgement formation. Reflective debrief after each.

6
Create · your engagement thesis & action plan

Define your perspective on where you invest, how, and why. Output: a personal engagement strategy and a concrete 12-month action plan.

The programme is experience-driven by design — you'll move from passive content into active engagement. Reflection moments are built in to translate insight into commitment.
