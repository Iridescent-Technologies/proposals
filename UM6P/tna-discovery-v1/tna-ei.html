1Welcome

Hello, I'm Zavmo.

This is a non-linear, adaptive pathway across Understand → Diagnose → Develop → Apply → Evaluate → Create. Before we shape your journey, we'll do two things: identify which kind of learner you are, then establish your individual entrepreneurial baseline through the EMP.

Why both: the EMP baseline is universal — it tells us where you are individually regardless of profile, academic level or self-reported expertise. Your profile then tells us which applied pathway to build on top of that baseline.

4EMP — universal baseline

Entrepreneurial Mindset Profile · personality traits.

This is the baseline we run for every learner regardless of profile, academic level or self-reported expertise. Rate each trait based on how strongly it shows up in your day-to-day. It's a starting snapshot — the full diagnostic happens in Stage 2 of the pathway.

Why universal: the EMP gives us a real picture of individual competencies that doesn't depend on what you've said about yourself elsewhere. Two students at the same level can have very different action orientation, risk acceptance and execution profiles — and the EMP is what reveals that.

8Your personalised path

Your adaptive, non-linear pathway.

Built on two foundations: your EMP baseline (universal individual competencies) and your learner profile (OCP employee in a technical / R&D function). Profile sets the applied context; EMP sets the developmental focus.

Six stages — your tailored sequence

1
Light touchUnderstand · entrepreneurship & innovation foundations

Framed for technical / R&D contributors in a corporate innovation setting — not for founders. Shared language first.

2
Your entryDiagnose · EMP outputs, mindset, team & org capability

Builds on your EMP baseline. Adds mindset mapping and connects to OCP's organisational innovation system.

3
FocusDevelop · risk acceptance & limited-structure tolerance

Targeted work on your two EMP development priorities — scenario-based, practical, with reflection.

4
Apply · your function-specific challenge

Bring your transformation initiative through trade-off frameworks, stakeholder mapping, and innovation system thinking — specific to OCP's environment.

5
Evaluate · assess outcomes & refine

Judgement under uncertainty in a corporate innovation context. What worked, what didn't, what to adjust.

6
Create · a contribution to OCP's innovation effort

A concrete recommendation, proposal or initiative artefact you can take into your team or function.

The pathway recalibrates as evidence accumulates. You may revisit Diagnose after Apply, or move directly from Develop to Create on parallel tracks.
