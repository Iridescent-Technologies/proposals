UM6P's One Court Africa programme is, structurally, a Bloom-progressed journey through six modules that move participants from foundational understanding of impact investing to a defined personal investment thesis and committed ecosystem engagement. The audience profile is distinctive — NBA players, athlete-leaders and emerging impact investors from non-traditional finance backgrounds — and the spec is explicit that the design must prioritise accessible-but-sophisticated investment learning, contextual interpretation, and identity formation as ecosystem contributors.

Zavmo's content graph already holds the substantive material for every module: investment fundamentals, social and impact entrepreneurship, venture analysis, due diligence, risk-return-impact trade-offs, strategic partnership models, and personal thesis development. Crucially, the graph holds this content at the right pedagogical register — not the dense actuarial language of CFA prep, but the practitioner-grade interpretation needed by high-influence, non-finance-native learners.

Section 04

Personalisation across the One Court Africa audience

The specification names a deliberately diverse audience — NBA players, athlete-leaders, emerging impact investors, ecosystem builders, graduate and executive learners, corporate innovation leaders. Three illustrative personas show how Zavmo routes each through their own version of the same programme.

Persona A · Active NBA Player

Marcus, 28 · 6 years in the league · investing actively

High influence and capital, low formal finance background. Strong opinions, time-poor, prefers conversation over lecture. Most engaged when shown live African ventures rather than abstract theory.

Adaptive path
  1. M1 at conversational depth · African case-first framing
  2. M2 deep · African ecosystem immersion content
  3. M3 with founder-pitch labs (not financial modelling)
  4. M4 simulation-first · IC role-play in cohort
  5. M5 with personal brand & influence overlay
  6. M6 personal thesis tied to existing portfolio
Persona B · Former Athlete with Foundation

James, 41 · retired · running a $20M family foundation

Already philanthropic, wants to evolve from grant-making to impact investing. Reasonable finance literacy. Needs structured rigour, not introductory framing.

Adaptive path
  1. Skip M1 · enter at M2 deep
  2. M3 + M4 full depth · L6 modules
  3. M5 with strategic partnership focus
  4. M6 thesis tied to foundation strategy
  5. Optional capstone: redesign of foundation investment policy
Persona C · Non-Traditional Emerging Investor

Ade, 36 · tech founder · first-time investor in African ventures

Operator background, strong on product and operations, gaps on investment process and portfolio thinking. Wants to angel-invest meaningfully, not just write cheques.

Adaptive path
  1. M1 light · skim foundations
  2. M2 with founder-empathy framing
  3. M3 + M4 full · with strong DD and risk focus
  4. M5 with operator-investor overlay
  5. M6 thesis built around angel-portfolio construction

Coverage strength & light-coverage areas
