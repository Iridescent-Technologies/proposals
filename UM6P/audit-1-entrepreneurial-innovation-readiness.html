UM6P's Entrepreneurial & Innovation Readiness pathway is, structurally, a Bloom's-anchored journey through 35 capability units spanning individual, team, organisational and systems levels. Zavmo's graph already holds the substantive content for every one of those 35 units — not as approximations, but as discrete, levelled, role-mapped modules drawn from the UK NOS framework and OFQUAL-recognised qualifications across RQF Levels 3 to 7. This document audits the match.

The implication for UM6P is direct: rather than commissioning original content for each capability area, the partnership inherits a content engine that already differentiates by learner role, prior experience and qualification level. UM6P retains the pedagogical wrapper (diagnostic flow, EMP integration, challenge framing) and contextual customisation (Moroccan and pan-African case material). Zavmo supplies the underlying capability content, granular xAPI tracking, and the personalisation logic that turns one course into many pathways.

Section 04

Personalisation in practice

The same pathway serves very different learners. Three illustrative personas demonstrate how Zavmo's graph routes each through their own version of the UM6P specification — using only the content already in the graph.

Persona A · Undergraduate

Yasmine, 21 · Engineering finalist · pre-startup

Entering with high passion, low structured-experience. EMP shows strong on Idea Generation and Action Orientation, weak on Risk Acceptance and Execution.

Adaptive path
  1. U1, U2, U3 at RQF 3–4 depth
  2. EMP diagnostic (D1) + Mindset Mapping (D2)
  3. Targeted DEV4 (Risk), DEV10 (Execution), DEV13 (Persistence)
  4. Skip DEV18, DEV19 (team/org modules)
  5. A1 + C1 Lean Canvas capstone
Persona B · Corporate Intrapreneur

Karim, 34 · Senior PM · launching a new business line

Strong execution and team competencies, weaker on Nonconformity and Future Focus. Needs corporate-innovation context, not startup-from-scratch.

Adaptive path
  1. Diagnostic-first entry: D1, D3, D4
  2. U2 (innovation as a system) at RQF 6 depth
  3. Targeted DEV3, DEV8, DEV16 (activation)
  4. DEV18 (team), DEV19 (org capability)
  5. A3 (org application) + C1 + C2 inside live business line
Persona C · PhD / Spin-out Founder

Léa, 29 · Materials science PhD · spin-out lead

High Future Focus and Idea Generation, low Interpersonal Sensitivity and Self-Confidence in commercial contexts. Needs venture creation depth.

Adaptive path
  1. Skip U1 — direct to U2, U3 at RQF 6
  2. Full D1–D4 diagnostic suite
  3. Targeted DEV11, DEV14, DEV17
  4. E1 deep-dive (opportunity evaluation)
  5. C1 full venture creation at RQF 7 + C3 portfolio

Section 06

Coverage strength & light-coverage areas

An honest summary of where Zavmo's existing graph is deep, and where contextual customisation will add the most value to UM6P.

Strong existing coverage

  • All 14 EMP variables — both personality traits and competencies — mapped to NOS, JDs and RQF qualifications
  • Mindset and growth-mindset content at RQF 3–6 depth
  • Team capability development including aggregated team diagnostics
  • OD and organisational capability content through to RQF 7
  • Idea-generation, design thinking and venture-creation primitives
  • Reflective practice, coaching and learning-loop content
  • Risk assessment, decision-making under uncertainty, systems thinking

Light coverage · candidate customisation

  • Moroccan and pan-African entrepreneurship case material — best authored with UM6P
  • UM6P-specific innovation horizons (H1/H2/H3) framing — light wrapper over existing content
  • EMP interpretation against UM6P graduate-attribute framework
  • Industry sector overlays (phosphate, agri, energy) — to be added per cohort
  • Capstone portfolio rubric calibrated to UM6P academic standards
  • Arabic-language UI option — extends existing Armenian/English/French capability
