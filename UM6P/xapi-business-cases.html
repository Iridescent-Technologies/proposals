Section 01 — Framing

Beyond completion · what the evidence layer is actually for

Every learning platform records completions. Zavmo's instrumentation goes further: it captures the texture of how a learner moved through the work — what they understood, when their confidence shifted, which trade-offs they could hold, where they got stuck, and what they could ultimately demonstrate. For UM6P, this matters in three practical ways.

Sponsor reporting

UM6P, OCP and One Court Africa each need defensible evidence that pilots changed how participants think and act — not just that seats were filled. The xAPI layer is what makes that claim auditable rather than anecdotal.

Cohort intelligence

Each programme has 2–3 distinct learner archetypes. Trackable signals separate the corporate-innovation cohort from the founder cohort, the sustainability strategist from the sales-aligned operator, the family-office principal from the angel-investing athlete.

Commercial follow-on

The same data identifies who is ready for the next thing — the accelerator seat, the cross-SBU recommendation, the LP commitment, the alumni mentor pairing. The pilot becomes a structured pipeline, not a one-off.

Section 03 — Programme 01 · Entrepreneurial & Innovation Readiness

EIR · turning 35 capability units into a venture pipeline

EIR is a Bloom-anchored journey across 35 units, anchored to the 14-variable Entrepreneurial Mindset Profile (EMP) and three learner archetypes — corporate innovators, idea-rich aspirant founders, and venture-creation operators. Each archetype has a different commercial follow-on, and the xAPI data is what tells UM6P which learner is which.

Three learner archetypes the data must separate

Corporate innovator

Section 06 — Cross-cutting

Four ROI levers that apply across all three programmes

Beyond programme-specific value, the same xAPI layer compounds across all three pilots in four reusable ways. These are particularly relevant when UM6P thinks about pricing, packaging and Cohort 02 economics.

1. Replace surveys with evidence

Every programme currently relies on post-programme self-report to justify sponsor and donor spend. The xAPI evidence layer replaces survey-based outcome reporting with auditable, learner-level evidence — defensible to ESG auditors, NBPA leadership, foundation boards and corporate sponsors alike.

2. Make the cohort a pipeline

The same signals that prove "the programme worked" also identify the top 10–15% of each cohort for follow-on actions: accelerator seats, LP commitments, cross-SBU promotions, alumni-mentor pairings. The cohort stops being a destination and becomes a structured pipeline.

3. Build an ownable IP layer

Validated capstone evidence — ventures, recommendations, theses — accumulates into an asset library UM6P can monetise: Cohort 02 content, partner-VC dealflow, sponsor case studies, recruiting collateral for the next intake.

4. Price the next cohort with data

SBU heatmaps, archetype distributions and dropout cliffs from Cohort 01 directly inform Cohort 02 pricing, packaging and seat allocation. The data underwrites premium pricing for what was previously a guess.

Section 07 — What we'd do next

From inventory to dashboards · a two-step path

The 24 verbs and 137 activity types are the supply side. The demand side — what UM6P, OCP and OCA actually want to see — is best fixed by working backwards from named KPIs per programme. We recommend two short workstreams.

  1. Discovery: name the 3–5 KPIs per programme (1 week). One working session per programme with the named sponsor (UM6P, OCP/SPS, OCA leadership). For each, agree the 3–5 questions the data must answer — venture pipeline yield, cross-SBU recommendations actioned, LP-ready alumni, etc. This is the contract between the evidence layer and the commercial case.
  2. Pilot dashboard build (2–3 weeks per programme). A live, programme-specific dashboard reading from Zavmo's xAPI layer. One view for the cohort lead (what's happening this week), one for the sponsor (what's happening over the programme), one for the post-cohort follow-on team (who is ready for what next).
  3. Cohort 02 pricing & packaging review. Once Cohort 01 evidence lands, a short readout that translates the data into Cohort 02 commercial recommendations — seat pricing, archetype targeting, SBU sequencing, sponsor-package tiers.
