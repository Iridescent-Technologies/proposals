SPS Academy · Sustainability, Resource Systems & Industrial Transformation

A unit-by-unit audit of Zavmo's content graph against UM6P's SPS Academy pilot specification for OCP. Eight-week pilot domain mapped to UK NOS, IEMA standards, OFQUAL-recognised qualifications and xAPI tracking.

UM6P's SPS Academy pilot is a developmentally tight specification: a two-layer architecture (enterprise-level domain understanding, then SBU-specific contextualisation), built on a six-stage Industrial Capability Development Loop, delivered in a 14–18-hour, 8-week envelope for full-time SPS professionals. Zavmo's graph already holds sustainability, resource-systems, resilience and industrial-transformation content at the precise rigour the specification calls for — globally grounded, IEMA-aligned, transferable across sectors before SPS context overlays.

What this audit shows is straightforward: every one of the 13 specified units is matched by existing Zavmo modules; the 8-week pacing maps cleanly onto adaptive module activation; and Zavmo's xAPI architecture can produce the evidence of contextual reasoning, trade-off judgement and contribution-oriented thinking that the specification names as primary success signals.

Section 04

Personalisation across the SPS workforce

The same 8-week pilot serves five very different SPS workforce groups. Below, three illustrative personas show how Zavmo routes each through their own version of the same UM6P specification.

Persona A · Operational / plant-adjacent

Hassan, 38 · Maintenance team leader, Jorf Lasfar

Limited prior exposure to ESG language. Strong operational expertise. Needs systems-awareness without engineering jargon.

Adaptive path
  1. U1, U2 at RQF 3–4 depth · plant-adjacent scenarios
  2. D1 light · D2 deep (resilience focus)
  3. DEV1 trade-off labs in maintenance scenarios
  4. A1 + A2 grounded in own work environment
  5. C1 recommendation tied to current shift
Persona B · Commercial & customer-facing

Sofia, 34 · Account manager, fertiliser markets

Customer-aware, sustainability is a sales conversation. Needs market and competitiveness depth more than operations.

Adaptive path
  1. U1 light · U3 deep (competitiveness focus, RQF 5)
  2. D1 market-pressure variant
  3. DEV1 stakeholder trade-off variant
  4. A1 customer-scenario variant
  5. C1 customer-conversation playbook output
Persona C · Transformation & strategy-adjacent

Karim, 45 · Strategic projects lead

High prior knowledge, needs Bloom 5–6 depth. Wants transformation pathway evaluation, not foundational definitions.

Adaptive path
  1. Skip U1 · U2, U3 at RQF 6–7 depth
  2. D1 + D2 full diagnostic suite
  3. DEV2 systems reasoning deep dive
  4. E1 + E2 transformation pathway evaluation
  5. C1 cross-SBU recommendation at portfolio level

Section 06

Coverage strength & light-coverage areas

An honest summary of where Zavmo's existing graph is deep and where contextual customisation will add value for SPS.

Strong existing coverage

  • IEMA-aligned sustainability content from foundation to practitioner
  • Resource-systems content covering energy, water, materials and logistics interdependencies
  • Systems thinking and trade-off reasoning at RQF 5–7
  • Operational resilience and business continuity (BCM-aligned)
  • Sustainable procurement (CIPS-aligned) and supply-chain resilience
  • Lean, Six Sigma and continuous improvement primitives
  • Decision-making under uncertainty and scenario planning
  • Industrial transformation, change management and OD content

Light coverage · candidate customisation

  • OCP-specific operational scenarios — best authored with SPS subject-matter leads
  • Phosphate and fertiliser value-chain context — sector overlay required
  • Moroccan regulatory and market context — supplemental content
  • SBU Academy Series enterprise/SBU two-layer routing — UM6P pedagogical scaffolding
  • OCP capstone rubric and assessment cadence — joint definition
  • French and Arabic language coverage — extends existing capability
