The idea

The best way to learn is one-to-one.
Now it's yours.

Forty years ago, the educational psychologist Benjamin Bloom showed that learners taught one-to-one, and only moved on once they'd truly mastered something, dramatically outperformed the same learners in a normal classroom. The catch: a personal tutor for everyone was never affordable.

Zavmo closes that gap. Instead of one lesson for everyone, you get a tutor that starts from who you are and where you're going — and teaches to that. No videos to sit through. No one-size quiz. A real conversation that adapts to every answer you give.

What this means for you: two people on Zavmo, learning the "same" topic, will have two genuinely different experiences — different examples, different pace, different depth — because it's shaped around each of them.

What Zavmo captures · and why

Your active learning is captured — so it counts

Zavmo doesn't just track whether you finished. As you learn, it records the meaningful moments — a question answered, a skill applied, a level of understanding reached, a reflection written — as open xAPI statements. xAPI (the "Experience API") is the international standard for learning records, so what you do here is captured in a recognised, portable form.

Reason one

So your learning is recognised

Every statement is mapped to the learning outcomes and assessment criteria behind your subject — and to formal frameworks. It's the evidence that you didn't just attend, you demonstrated it. That's what turns effort into a recognised outcome.

Reason two

So the teaching adapts to you

The same signals tell your tutor how you're really doing — where you flew, where you struggled — so the next lesson meets you exactly there. Capturing your active learning is how one-to-one teaching stays truly personal.

Yours, and in your control

You can see what Zavmo holds — and it's kept safe

Capturing your learning only works if it's transparent. So it is.

  • You can see it. Your own learning record and reflections are yours to view — what you've covered, what you've mastered, and what Zavmo remembers about you.
  • It's about learning, not surveillance. The record captures how you're progressing — not private free-text — and exists to recognise your effort and improve your teaching.
  • It's handled responsibly. Records are kept securely and pseudonymised, in line with UM6P's data protection and GDPR obligations.
  • It's built to a standard. Because it's xAPI, mapped to Ofqual, NOS and Bloom's, your evidence is recognised beyond Zavmo — it belongs to your learning, not to one app.
