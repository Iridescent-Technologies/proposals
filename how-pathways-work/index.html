Set up a pathway in 16 steps

A pathway is a programme of learning for a group of people. You build it in nine chapters, in the row across the top. Open the builder beside this page and follow the steps in order.

  1. Click Learning pathways, then New pathway, and name it.
  2. Work through the nine chapters, left to right.
  3. Purpose: check what should change at work.
  4. Leave Compliance pathway off, unless everyone must do everything.
  5. Learners: check who gets it.
  6. Sources: tick qualifications and drop in your own files.
  7. Outcomes: clear anything marked Needs you.
  8. Discover: check the skills check at the start.
  9. Discuss: choose an end date, or none.
  10. Deliver: check the task they do at work.
  11. Demonstrate: choose who signs off.
  12. See and do: approve the badges.
  13. Preview: walk it as a learner.
  14. Launch: choose who gets it, by when, and your reports.
  15. After: check the follow-up a month later.
  16. See where each learner started and finished, in Skills.

Step 1 of 16 · Start

Start a new pathway

What you do

  1. Click Learning pathways in the left menu.
  2. Click New pathway.
  3. Type its name under What is this pathway called?, then click Start it, beside it.
  4. Answer Zavmo’s first question: who is it for, and what should change at work? Click Talk to me, or type in Or type to me.
  5. Zavmo fills Purpose as you talk. Click Open Purpose to check it, or Undo.

You will know it worked when the pathway opens as a Draft on Purpose, and the header counts up from 0 of 9 chapters ready.

Why: Zavmo fills every chapter with our recommendation. You only change what you disagree with.

Step 2 of 16 · Start

Move between the chapters

What you do

  1. Click a chapter name in the row to open it. Work from Purpose to After.
  2. A tick means the chapter is ready. An empty ring means it is still to check.
  3. Click Preview as a learner at any time to see what a learner sees.

You will know it worked when the header counts up, for example 2 of 9 chapters ready.

Why: each chapter answers one question, from why the pathway exists to whether it changed what people do.

Step 3 of 16 · Chapter 1

Purpose

What you do

  1. Click Purpose in the chapter row.
  2. Open What should change at work. Check the answers to What should people do differently at work? and What will show it worked? Type over anything you would change.
  3. Open Organisation context, marked To check, and correct it.

You will know it worked when every row says Ready and Purpose has a tick.

Why: a pathway that names a change at work can be measured.

Step 4 of 16 · In the header

Adapt to each learner, or compliance

What you do

  1. Find Compliance pathway: Off in the dark header, beside Preview as a learner.
  2. Leave it Off for learning that adapts. Then switch each choice in Deliver On or Off.
  3. Turn it On only for training everyone must do in full, such as fire safety.
  4. With it on, read the banner. Each choice that adapts is locked, with a padlock.

You will know it worked when the banner says Compliance pathway: every learner does everything.

Why: adapting is how people build skill. They skip what they can already do and start at their own level.

Step 5 of 16 · Chapter 2

Learners

What you do

  1. Click Learners in the chapter row.
  2. Open each row marked To check: By job role, By department or location and What they must know before they start.
  3. Type over anything that is wrong, for example Languages they learn in.
  4. Changed your mind? Click Undo beside the change.

You will know it worked when each row says Ready, and a row you changed says 1 change by you.

Why: the same content lands differently for a new starter and an expert. Learners set their own needs, such as captions, in learn.

Step 6 of 16 · Chapter 3

Sources

What you do

  1. Click Sources in the chapter row.
  2. Under Describe the pathway or type a subject, type what the pathway covers.
  3. Tick or untick the matches, and the Future Fluency units below them. Zavmo has ticked its picks.
  4. Have your own material? Drag it onto Drop files here, or click choose files.
  5. Leave Teach only from these sources On.

You will know it worked when your files show under Your material, each with Teach from this on.

Why: Zavmo teaches only what it can point to. It searches over 1,000 recognised qualifications from over 80 awarding bodies, the organisations that set and certify qualifications.

Step 7 of 16 · Chapter 4

Outcomes

What you do

  1. Click Outcomes in the chapter row.
  2. Under Show, click the module that needs you, such as Module 3.
  3. Open Outcomes and criteria, marked Needs you. The criterion nothing checks says Never assessed.
  4. Under How should I fix it?, click Use this on the fix you want. Zavmo marks the one it recommends.
  5. Open Learning objectives, marked To check, and read the two Zavmo drafted.

You will know it worked when Outcomes and criteria says Ready, and the page says Nothing here needs you.

Why: every lesson and every piece of evidence traces back to an outcome. A criterion is one thing a learner must show. Each has a Bloom level: how deep the task goes, from remembering a fact to creating something new.

Step 8 of 16 · Chapter 5, Teaching, stage 1 of 4

Discover

What you do

  1. Click Teaching in the chapter row.
  2. Click Discover in the row of four stages.
  3. Open Questions for this pathway and check the three questions.
  4. Open Skills check at the start and check the skills it asks about.
  5. Questions every learner answers once, such as job title, sit in Settings. Click it to change those.

You will know it worked when both rows say Ready.

Why: the skills check is a short training needs assessment. A learner skips any module they already pass.

Step 9 of 16 · Teaching, stage 2 of 4

Discuss

What you do

  1. Click Discuss in the row of four stages.
  2. Open Discussion questions and check the four questions about their role.
  3. Leave Learners book their own sessions On. They choose their own time.
  4. Under Complete by, click No end date or A date.

You will know it worked when Discussion questions says Ready.

Why: their answers shape every example, so the learning is about their own job. You decide whether there is a date to finish by.

Step 10 of 16 · Teaching, stage 3 of 4

Deliver

What you do

  1. Click Deliver in the row of four stages.
  2. Open Modules and lessons and check the modules.
  3. Open Apply it at work, marked To check. Check the one real task they do after each module.
  4. Turn Lessons in order or Every module required On only if everyone must do the same.
  5. Leave the other switches On, unless one gets in your people’s way.

You will know it worked when Apply it at work says Ready.

Why: lessons begin where the learner is. Low confidence gets more practice.

Step 11 of 16 · Teaching, stage 4 of 4

Demonstrate

What you do

  1. Click Demonstrate in the row of four stages.
  2. Open Sign-off, marked Needs you.
  3. Pick who signs off: Their line manager, Someone in L&D (your learning and development team) or An assessor you name.
  4. Open Evidence the learner chooses and Questions with a number answer, and check both.

You will know it worked when the Demonstrate stage no longer says Needs you.

Why: sign-off is the only way to a certificate. Learners show they can do the work, not only that they know it.

Step 12 of 16 · Chapter 6

See and do

What you do

  1. Click See and do in the chapter row.
  2. Under Games in each module, untick any game you do not want. Click See every game to try them.
  3. Open Pictures for objectives, outcomes and criteria, marked To check.
  4. Under Badges, click Approve on each badge, or Approve all 6.

You will know it worked when Badges this pathway gives no longer says 0 of 6 approved. Launch waits for the badges.

Why: practice and pictures make learning stick. A badge you do not approve is not given.

Step 13 of 16 · Chapter 7

Preview

What you do

  1. Click Preview as a learner, at the top right of any chapter. It is marked 3 in step 2.
  2. You see the lesson as a learner does. Answer as they would, in Your answer.
  3. Try the replies a learner has, such as Got it, Slower please or Show me an example.
  4. Check What we’re covering matches the outcome you set.

You will know it worked when Launch opens. It opens once you have walked it.

Why: you catch what would confuse a learner before anyone else sees it. Tell Zavmo anything that confused you, and it changes it. Nothing you do here counts as learning or shows in reports.

Step 14 of 16 · Chapter 8

Launch

What you do

  1. Click Launch in the chapter row.
  2. Under Who gets it, click Change to give it to people, a group or a department.
  3. Leave Required On, pick a date under Due by, and click Choose under Certificate and cover.
  4. Under Reports, switch on what you want to see once it is live, such as Progress and completion.
  5. Click Launch version 1.

You will know it worked when the version is locked, so every learner gets the same pathway. People already on it stay on their version.

To change it after launch: duplicate it as a new draft, edit that, and launch it as the next version.

Why: learners get it on the day you choose, with a clear date to finish by. Zavmo alerts you if anything needs a look once it is live.

Step 15 of 16 · Chapter 9

After

What you do

  1. Click After in the chapter row.
  2. Leave One question: what did you think? On.
  3. Leave Manager check-in at the end On.
  4. Open Follow-up after a month. Check When, Skills rated again by and What they are asked.

You will know it worked when Follow-up after a month says Ready and After has a tick.

Why: people forget most of what they do not use. The answers go to Reports, beside where they started and finished.

Step 16 of 16 · After launch

Check the result in Skills

What you do

  1. Click Skills in the left menu.
  2. Click In your pathways and open your pathway. Check the skills each module builds.
  3. Click Assessments to see where learners started and finished.
  4. Click People and open a learner, such as Priya Shah. The spider shows where she started and finished this pathway, and every rating sits below it.

You will know it worked when your pathway lists its skills, and does not say None linked.

Why: Skills records the same skills three times, so the next pathway starts where this one ended.

  1. StartDiscoverThe skills check places the learner.
  2. FinishDemonstrateThe same skills are checked again, for example by their manager.
  3. A month laterAfterThey rate the skills again, once used at work.
