Set-up guide

Set up Skills and assessments

These screens show Skills as we are building it.

Set up Skills in 12 steps, then use it every week

Skills is one record of what each person can do. You say which skills matter, we find out where each person is, and you act on the gap.

  1. Click Skills in the left menu.
  2. Click Frameworks and pick one way to start.
  3. Check the skills, and the line for each level.
  4. Click Choose priority skills and set a target for each.
  5. Click Roles, then New role profile, and approve it.
  6. Click Assessments, then New assessment.
  7. Check each section of Zavmo’s draft.
  8. Read the line for each answer.
  9. Choose who rates under Who rates.
  10. Click Publish, then Yes, publish it.
  11. Put it at Discover in a pathway.
  12. Click In your pathways and check the skills each module builds.

Then, every week: act on the cards under What to do next, and read the skills picture.

Step 1 of 21 · Set up Skills

Open Skills

What you do

  1. Click Skills in the left menu.
  2. Skills opens on Overview. The tabs beside it are People, Roles, Assessments, Frameworks and In your pathways.
  3. Ask Zavmo a question in its conversation, such as "Do we have the skills we need for the AI plan?"

You will know it worked when you see What to do next and The skills picture.

Why: the next pathway starts where the last one ended, and your people lead can answer the board.

Step 2 of 21 · Set up Skills

Start your skills framework

What you do

  1. Click the Frameworks tab.
  2. Pick one way to start: Start from ours, Import a CSV or Draft from pathways.
  3. Importing a spreadsheet? Click Download the layout first, so your rows match.

You will know it worked when Zavmo drafts the framework and opens it, marked Draft.

Why: a skills framework lists the skills your organisation asks for, and what each one looks like at each level. Whichever way you start, it becomes your one primary framework.

Step 3 of 21 · Set up Skills

Check your skills framework

What you do

  1. Click Check the skills in Zavmo’s reply.
  2. Click Change beside any skill, or Add a skill.
  3. Open What each level looks like, marked To check.
  4. Read the line for each level: Own work, Team, Department and Organisation.
  5. To link a pathway you built, find Pathways that build it under the open skill and click Attach a pathway. Pick your pathway, then check the modules Zavmo suggests.
  6. Already have your own framework? Under Mapped onto this one, click Upload a framework and choose your spreadsheet, CSV or document. Check any skill Zavmo is unsure of.

You will know it worked when no section says To check. Publish then opens, and the framework is marked Primary.

Why: every rater sees the same line for each level, so a rating means the same thing to everyone.

Step 4 of 21 · Set up Skills

Choose your priority skills

What you do

  1. On Overview, click Choose priority skills, above the first bar.
  2. Tick the four or five skills your plans depend on.
  3. For each one, pick Level, Practice and Who needs it.
  4. Click Save 4 priority skills.

You will know it worked when the Overview lists them under Priority skills, set by you.

Why: a target is a level and a practice word, such as “Team, uses it routinely”. The skills picture and Download for the board lead with these.

Step 5 of 21 · Set up Skills

Make a role profile from our library

What you do

  1. Click the Roles tab, then New role profile.
  2. Type the role into the search, such as Operations team lead.
  3. Pick the closest job description. Click Read it to see it in full.
  4. Open Zavmo's draft. Check each Skill, Target and Need. Click Change a skill to change one.
  5. Fill in Owner and review date, then click Approve the profile.

You will know it worked when the draft no longer says To check. Nothing is live until you approve it.

Why: a role profile says which skills a role needs, and how well. We built our library from over 43,000 job descriptions, so you do not start from scratch. Your own job description is optional: Choose a file.

Step 6 of 21 · Set up Skills

Start an assessment

What you do

  1. Click the Assessments tab.
  2. Click New assessment. Or ask Zavmo: "Build a starting assessment for team leads, from their profile."
  3. To finish one Zavmo has drafted already, open it under Drafts.

You will know it worked when a draft opens with five sections to check.

Why: an assessment finds out where someone is on a skill. A person is asked once, not on every pathway.

Step 7 of 21 · Set up Skills

Check Zavmo's draft

What you do

  1. Tell Zavmo who it is for, as in the request at the top.
  2. Click Check the question in Zavmo's reply.
  3. Open Skills it asks about and check the skills.
  4. Open When it is asked and What it writes, and check both.

You will know it worked when only the sections in the next two steps still say To check. Publish opens once none do.

Why: Zavmo takes the skills from the profile and drafts the questions. You do not write them from scratch.

Step 8 of 21 · Set up Skills

Check the question

What you do

  1. Read the line under Knows it, Has applied it and Uses it routinely. Learners answer against these lines.
  2. Keep Not yet, or not sure, so nobody has to guess.
  3. Check the three answers under How sure are you?
  4. Tell Zavmo any line you would change.

You will know it worked when The question says Ready.

Why: we ask about practice, not knowledge. People answer "have you done this at work?" well. Confidence sits beside the answer and is never turned into a level.

Step 9 of 21 · Set up Skills

Choose who rates

What you do

  1. Open Who rates, marked To check.
  2. Leave The learner rates themselves On.
  3. Leave Their manager confirms On. Zavmo proposes a level and the manager agrees or changes it.
  4. Leave Two quick questions before a skip On, so nobody skips a lesson on their word alone.

You will know it worked when Who rates says Ready and Publish opens.

Why: self-ratings are a good start and a poor proof. The level that counts is the one shown at work.

Step 10 of 21 · Set up Skills

Publish the assessment

What you do

  1. Click Publish. It opens once every section says Ready.
  2. Click Yes, publish it.
  3. Click Use it in a pathway.

You will know it worked when it moves to Published and says You published this today.

Why: once it is published you can use it at Discover or Demonstrate on any pathway. A later change makes version 2. Answers already given stay as they are.

Step 11 of 21 · Set up Skills

Put it at the start of a pathway

What you do

  1. Open Discover: assessment at the start. Only published assessments are listed.
  2. Pick the assessment, such as Where do you start? Team leads.
  3. Check Demonstrate: assessment at the end. It asks the same skills again.

You will know it worked when the row says Ready, with You chose this and an Undo.

Why: Discover places each learner, so they skip what they can already do. Demonstrate shows the change at the end.

See Discover and see Demonstrate in the pathway guide.

Step 12 of 21 · Set up Skills

Link skills to your pathways

What you do

  1. Click the In your pathways tab.
  2. Open a pathway, such as Future Fluency, Level 3.
  3. Check the Skills this builds for each module.
  4. Open a pathway marked To check and check the skills Zavmo linked.
  5. For one marked None linked, such as the refresher, click Ask Zavmo to link the refresher.

You will know it worked when no pathway says None linked.

Why: without this link a pathway cannot skip what someone has shown, or record what moved.

Step 13 of 21 · Use it every week

Act on What to do next

What you do

  1. On Overview, read the cards under What to do next. The most useful comes first.
  2. Click the button on a card: Give a pathway, Draft a pathway, Review or Remind them.
  3. Click the cross on a card to set it aside.
  4. Click the link under the cards to see the rest.

You will know it worked when Zavmo shows what it did in the conversation, with an Undo.

Why: each card says what its figure rests on. Nothing changes until you press the button.

Step 14 of 21 · Use it every week

Read the skills picture

What you do

  1. Under Showing, pick the whole organisation, or a department, location, role or team.
  2. Under Change since, pick the date to compare with.
  3. On a skill below target, click Give a pathway.
  4. Click Find people to see who already has it.
  5. Click Download for the board for one page on your priority skills.

You will know it worked when the line beside the boxes shows how many people you are looking at.

Why: each bar splits people into Shown at work, at target, Rated at target, not shown, Below target and No rating.

Step 15 of 21 · Use it every week

Close a role's gaps

What you do

  1. Click the Roles tab, then open a role, such as Team lead.
  2. Open Zavmo proposes a change, if there is one, and check it.
  3. Select a part of any bar to see who is in it.
  4. Click Give the gap pathways to 19 people. Each person gets the pathway for the skill they are below on.
  5. Under Ready for this role, click Add to a shortlist.

You will know it worked when the person says On the shortlist, with an Undo.

Why: Ready for this role lists people in other roles who show the essentials. That is where succession starts.

Step 16 of 21 · Use it every week

Find who can do this

What you do

  1. Click Who can do this? at the top of Skills.
  2. Pick the skill under Skill, and the lowest level you need under At least.
  3. Narrow it by Department, Location or Role. Leave Shown at work only on.
  4. Click Add to a shortlist beside each person, then Download the shortlist.

You will know it worked when the shortlist box names how many people it holds.

Why: each result says how the level is known, and when. Nothing is sent to anyone from this screen.

Step 17 of 21 · Use it every week

Open a person's record

What you do

  1. Click People in the left menu, then a person.
  2. Read each skill's ratings side by side: Who rated, Practice, Confidence and Date.
  3. Ask Zavmo what they should do next.
  4. Click a next step under Next for Priya, such as Talk to Hannah.

You will know it worked when a green line under the skill says what you set, with an Undo.

Why: we never average ratings into one number. The level that counts is the one shown at work.

Step 18 of 21 · Use it every week

Managers confirm a level

This is what a manager sees. You send a reminder from the Remind them card.

What the manager does

  1. Read where Zavmo's level came from, under each skill.
  2. Agree, or pick the level they have seen: Knows it, Has applied it or Uses it routinely.
  3. Click Agree, or I have not seen this.
  4. Click Next: Tom Okafor, 2 skills to move to the next person.

You will know it worked when the skill says You agreed, with an Undo.

Why: managers skip blank forms, so we never send one. It takes about a minute.

Step 19 of 21 · Use it every week

Compare the start and the end

What you do

  1. Click the Assessments tab.
  2. Open the end assessment, such as What changed? Team leads.
  3. Compare where people started and finished in the spider chart: the dashed outline is the start, the filled shape the end. The table below it gives At the start, At the end and Shown at work for each skill.
  4. Open A month later, at work to see when the follow-up is due.

You will know it worked when you see the three columns side by side. We never combine them into one score.

Why: a skip at the start is "placed", never "shown". It counts as shown only once they do it at work.

Step 20 of 21 · Use it every week

Read one skill's ratings

What you do

  1. In a person's record, find the skill.
  2. Read each row: Who rated, then Practice, Confidence and Date.
  3. Set a task by mistake? Click Undo beside it.

You will know it worked when you can see how the skill moved, row by row.

Why: a rating is never edited. A new one is added beside it, and the next pathway reads the record first.

Step 21 of 21

On a phone

On a phone, Skills is for reading. The priority skills and the cards are there, on the move. You make changes on a computer.
