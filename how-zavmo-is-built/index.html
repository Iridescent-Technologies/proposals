The model

A small model, purpose-built for learning.

Zavmo doesn't rent a general-purpose frontier LLM and hope it behaves. Saraswati is built on Microsoft Phi-3 Mini — small enough to run efficiently, tuned specifically for learning science, and on an MIT licence that's fully commercial-friendly.

Control

Behaviour you can govern

A model you tune is a model you can audit, test and constrain — not a black box whose behaviour changes when a vendor ships an update.

Per-market fit

One base, many markets

A lightweight LoRA adapter per market carries the local language register and qualification framework. UK is live; Ireland, Singapore and Australia follow the same pattern — no retraining from scratch.

Sustainability

Small by design

An SLM costs a fraction of a frontier LLM to run, per interaction — which is what makes one-to-one coaching affordable at organisational scale.

Grounded, not guessing

Retrieval over recall

Subject knowledge is retrieved at run time from the standards graph and character corpora, not baked into weights — so content stays current and traceable.

The knowledge foundation

Every lesson is built against a real standard.

The standards graph is a Neo4j knowledge base linking 601,000+ qualifications and their underlying skills across more than 150 countries and 30+ frameworks. It's the difference between generic content and content mapped to the exact competency your role and jurisdiction require.

NOS

Skills, from the framework

National Occupational Standards define what good looks like for a role — the skills and behaviours a competent person demonstrates.

OFQUAL

Content, from the qualification

Regulated qualifications supply the learning content and assessment criteria — the structured curriculum that leads to a credential.

MAP

Role → standard → lesson

Zavmo maps a learner's role to the right skills, pulls the matching qualification content, and generates a bespoke path — grounded, not invented.

The personalisation engine

Grounded in who the learner actually is.

Before Saraswati generates a single word, the LearnerSignalBundle assembles what's known about the learner — their role, prior interactions, stated goals, confidence, and neurodiversity preferences — and grounds the response in it. Personalisation isn't a greeting with a first name; it's the model reasoning from the real person.

Characters & orchestration

Specialists, conducted by an invisible thirteenth.

Rather than one voice trying to do everything, Zavmo fields seven specialist teaching characters — each tuned to a level of Bloom's taxonomy — plus a group-work mode where four team-mate avatars simulate a real workplace team. Agent 13 is the meta-agent above them: it reads a live flow-state signal each turn and picks the right specialist to keep the learner in the productive zone between boredom and overwhelm.

The measurement spine

Every interaction becomes evidence.

Learning that can't be measured can't be proven to the board. Zavmo emits xAPI statements — the open standard for learning data — for meaningful interactions, into a self-hosted yetanalytics lrsql Learning Record Store (Postgres-backed, Apache-2.0). It's wire-standard and vendor-neutral, so the same data can flow to your LRS too.

StandardxAPI 1.0.3 and 2.0.0 — portable, future-proof, not a proprietary format
StoreSelf-hosted yetanalytics lrsql, backed by Postgres — one service, actively maintained
ForwardingVendor-agnostic wire format; point statements at your own LRS via a single endpoint variable
FeedsAdmin reporting, learner insight (flow, learning personality), and market-level model improvement
Security & governance

Built to pass your security review.

The features win the demo; this is the section that gets the deal signed. Zavmo is engineered against recognised standards from the start, not bolted on afterwards.

ISO 27001

Information security

Engineered to the international information-security management standard — access control, encryption, and auditability as first-class requirements.

ISO 42001

Responsible AI

Built to the AI-management standard: governed model behaviour, documented decisions, and accountability for how the AI is trained and used.

Data residency

Your data, your region

Architecture supports EU data residency, so learner data stays where your compliance team needs it to.

Tenant isolation

Org-scoped by default

Every query is scoped to the organisation. Admins see their people and no one else's — isolation is enforced in the data layer, not just the UI.

Your data isn't the training set

Aggregate, not individual

Model improvement uses aggregated, market-level learning signals — never individual records — and character knowledge is retrieved (RAG), not baked into weights.

Guardrails

Grounded generation

Responses are grounded in the standards graph and quality-scored; the admin Prompt Studio lets teams test prompt changes in simulation before they ever reach a learner.
