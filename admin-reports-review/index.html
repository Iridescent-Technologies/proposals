Reports: what an admin sees, and what we recommend

Reports is where a client sees what their learning did: who started, what they can now do, and what changed at work. This page walks every report, shows where each figure comes from, and recommends what to change. Each recommendation has a number, so you can sign them off one at a time.

What we found

Every report reads the client's own records, which is right, and the two reports SLM gates follow it: EMP shows only where the client has EMP, and tutor allowance shows only where it is switched on. The weakness is that a pathway cannot choose its reports yet. The signed Launch step has seven report switches; none is built, so every report shows for every pathway. Three of the seven reports do not exist yet. And outcomes are reported twice, in Mind Palace and in Outcomes.

Screens are from the local review copy: today's main with every open pathway and Skills change, UM6P demonstration data, and the three build switches live has on. They show what UM6P's admin will see. Each is shown at laptop width (1366) and phone width (375). Broken things come first. Recommendations marked Before Monday are the ones UM6P's admin would notice; the rest can follow.

Screen 6 of 9 · What learners can do

Goals, criteria and outcomes

Goals shows every live pathway's purpose and each learner's own goal. Criterion by criterion shows the evidence against each criterion, with each learner's answer. Outcomes shows which outcomes are met.

Goals and Criterion by criterion show learners' own words to admins and managers without checking the client's "Managers see learning detail" switch in SLM. The person page does check it. On um6p-sandbox the switch is on, so UM6P sees no difference on Monday; for a client with it off, learners' words would show where they should not.

Outcomes and Mind Palace report the same thing in two places.

What we recommend

  1. R4Done: Goals and Criterion by criterion follow "Managers see learning detail" (Zavmo-v2 #3553, closed 24 Oct). #3553Done
  2. R5Mind Palace becomes Outcomes, as decided: one report, and the old address opens Outcomes. #3256After Monday

Screen 7 of 9 · What changed

Growth and the Mindset Profile

Growth shows growth moments by period, group or team. It cannot follow a pathway's choice, because no growth record names a pathway; Then and now, its second half, is not built.

The EMP report follows SLM: it exists only where the client has EMP, and then only for pathways that use it. The pathway's EMP choice sits on Purpose; the signed Launch step draws it among the report switches.

What we recommend

  1. R6Move the pathway's EMP choice to Launch, beside the other report switches. #3262After Monday
  2. R7Build the reports the signed list has and we do not: Then and now, Engagement and reaction, Skills against your framework, and the weekly summary by email. #3269 #3271 #3325 #3275After Monday
