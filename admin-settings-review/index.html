Settings: what an admin sees, and what we recommend

Settings is where SLM and the client's admin meet. SLM holds what Zavmo decides for a client: who they are, who controls their data, what they are allowed to use. The client's admin holds how they run it day to day. This page walks every Settings screen, shows where each value comes from, and recommends what to change. Each recommendation has a number, so you can sign them off one at a time.

What we found

Most of Settings already follows the rule. Client details are held in SLM and shown locked, marked Set by Zavmo. Team and roles, sign-in rules and conversation retention are the client's own. The weakness is in four places. Branding is not shown at all. Two contact emails and two language settings sit side by side, one from SLM and one from admin. Three profile fields are saved and never used. And nothing tells a client's admin what Zavmo has switched on for them, or changed for them.

These screens are the admin as it is built today on main, drawn with sample people and a sample client rather than a real one, because the live captures need you to sign in. They show the same controls UM6P's admin will see. Each is shown at laptop width (1366) and phone width (375). Live screens from um6p-sandbox.admin.zavmo.ai replace them once you have signed in. Recommendations marked Before Monday are the ones UM6P's admin would notice; the rest can follow.

Client details: what Zavmo holds for the client

The top of Settings shows what Zavmo holds for this client in SLM, locked: the client's name and learning address, region and language, who can join, Little Zav, the data controller and privacy contact, the support contact and the tutor allowance. An admin can read these and cannot change them. That is the rule working.

Three things are missing. The client's branding, their logo, font and colours, is not shown here at all. Little Zav shows On or Off but not which of its four parts the client has. And nowhere can an admin see which features Zavmo has switched on for them, so when a setting is missing they cannot tell whether it is off or not built.

What we recommend

  1. S1Show the client's branding in Client details, locked, marked Set by Zavmo: logo, font and the four colours. Later, let the admin change it within SLM's allowed fonts and colours. #3494Before Monday
  2. S2Let the client's admin change the support contact. It is theirs to run. The data controller and privacy contact stay locked in SLM, because they are legal. #3495After Monday
  3. S3Add a locked list, "What you have", of the features Zavmo has switched on for the client, in SLM's plain names. A#779After Monday
  4. S4Under Little Zav, show which parts the client has: Chat, Play, Help and Impact. Show Little Zav in the rail only where SLM has it on. A#777After Monday

Screen 2 of 8 · Organisation

Organisation profile: what the client fills in

Below Client details, the admin fills in their own profile: organisation name, trading name, time zone, default locale, contact email and registered address. It sits on the same screen as Client details, above.

This is where the duplicates are. "Default locale" offers about 25 languages and regions, but only US English changes anything, and it sits under SLM's Language. "Contact email" sits beside SLM's support contact, so a client has two. Trading name and Registered address are saved and nothing reads them. Time zone is read only to count Settings as finished, while Today splits overnight at a fixed 18:00.

What we recommend

  1. S5Rename "Default locale" to "Spelling", with two choices: UK English or US English. Language stays in SLM. A#746Before Monday
  2. S6Remove "Contact email". The support contact is the one contact, and S2 makes it the admin's to change. #3555After Monday
  3. S7Remove Trading name and Registered address, since nothing uses them. Keep Time zone, and use it: for Today's overnight split and for dates in reports and emails. #3555After Monday
  4. S8One name for the client, SLM's. Take the editable organisation name off the profile, and show SLM's name on Account too. #3245After Monday

Screen 4 of 8 · People who run it

Security and sign-in

Read only: the password rule, how long a sign-in lasts, and who must enter an emailed code. The rules are the same for every client, which is right.

Who must enter a code is a list of email addresses kept in a server setting. A client cannot see it, SLM cannot set it, and someone has to remember to add each new admin. Single sign-on is not built; this screen says so in a "Not built yet" banner.

What we recommend

  1. S9Make the sign-in code one rule for everyone: every admin and every Zavmo operator enters an emailed code. Retire the list of emails. #3556After Monday
  2. S10Turn "Admin previews" off for UM6P in SLM before Monday, so their admin sees no "Not built yet" banners. It is on for um6p-sandbox today. This is a switch in SLM, not code.Before Monday

Screen 5 of 8 · Zavmo and data

Zavmo and data

What Zavmo's conversation may do in this admin, how long conversations are kept, and connected content libraries.

One switch here is functionality rather than a preference: "I am on for this organisation" turns Zavmo's conversation on or off for the whole client, and SLM has no say. Everything else here is the client's own. Connecting, removing or uploading to a content library writes no audit record, which ISO 27001 needs.

What we recommend

  1. S11Zavmo's conversation becomes an SLM allowance, like every other feature: SLM allows it for a client, and the client's admin can still turn it off.After Monday
  2. S12Every content library connect, remove and upload writes an audit record: who, what, when and from where. #3552After Monday

Screen 6 of 8 · Zavmo and data

History

Two lists: Releases, which is what changed in the admin and when, and the audit log, which is what people at the client changed.

Two things a client should see are missing. What Zavmo changed for them in SLM, a feature switched on, a privacy contact corrected, is not in their audit log. And when Zavmo holds a client's updates in SLM, their admin is not told. SLM also calls that hold "Releases", a second thing with the same name.

What we recommend

  1. S13Show Zavmo's own changes for the client in their audit log, marked as made by Zavmo. #2920After Monday
  2. S14Rename the admin's Releases to "What's new". When Zavmo holds the client's updates, say so, locked, with the reason. A#782After Monday
