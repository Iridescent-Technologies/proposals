Layer 3 · Degree of fluencyWhere do you stand?

For each Fluency, a person is somewhere on a path. It runs from foundational (you have it) to functional (your role uses it now) to future (it is coming for you). The same skill is "future" for one person and "foundational" for another.

Personal by design. A relation between a Fluency and a person, never a folder.

Layer 2, the ten Fluencies, is Zavmo's own analysis. It comes from a first-party reading of the UK role corpus, and it is validated against live market signal. It is framed as "the market appears to be converging on…", never "the market needs."

Layer 3 · your degree of fluency

The same Fluency is "future" for one person and "foundational" for another.

Degree of fluency is not a folder a skill lives in. It is a property of the relationship between a Fluency and a person. Take AI Fluency and the skill of prompt engineering inside it:

Finance director

future. Not yet fluent. A growth edge coming for them.

Data analyst

functional. Working fluency. Part of doing the job now.

New graduate / digital native

foundational. Already fluent. An assumed baseline they arrived with.

Same Fluency, three degrees, one moment. There is no "future skills" folder. "Is this a future skill?" has no answer until you name whose future. You grow along a Fluency, from future → functional → foundational, the way you grow more fluent in a language.

A framework you can build a workforce on.

Layer 1 is the gate every other framework skips. Layer 2 is the map. Layer 3 is personal and moving. Together they turn "grow your people" from a slogan into a system.
