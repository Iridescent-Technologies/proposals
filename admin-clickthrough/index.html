Every admin screen, and where its settings come from

This is every screen in the Zavmo admin today, in the order an admin meets them. Under each screen is every setting and control on it, and the one place its value comes from. We hold each one to a simple rule. SLM decides what a client is allowed to use. The admin decides how each pathway uses it. Everything else is the same for every client, from one code base.

What we found

We are not there yet. We traced 276 settings and controls. 30 are held in SLM, and 38 are chosen per pathway, which is right. But 11 are build switches with no SLM row at all, and 38 are fixed in code. A few are held in SLM and in admin at once, which is the confusion the rule exists to stop. Against the signed walkthroughs, 91 match, 75 differ, 39 are not built and 71 have no signed screen yet. Every gap has a ticket: 19 were opened for this page, and the rest were already open.

The screens were captured read-only on um6p-sandbox.admin.zavmo.ai on 23 October 2026: a demonstration client with invented people. Every client runs the same build, so admin.zavmo.ai shows the same screens under its own brand. Each screen is shown at laptop width (1366) and phone width (375). Tap a screen to see it full length.

Screen 1 of 26 · Around every screen

The frame around every screen

Every screen sits in three panes: the rail on the left, the conversation with Zavmo in the middle, the work on the right. The client's name, logo and colours come from SLM, through one branding call when the admin opens.

Which places appear in the rail depends on two things. The person's role decides most of it. SLM decides the rest: Skills shows only where SLM has Skills management on. Little Zav is the exception. It shows for every admin, even where SLM has it off (A#777, #3554).

Three switches that shape the whole frame, the three panes, People and Reports, are set in the build file rather than in SLM. They are on for every client, so they decide nothing, but they keep dead pages alive. We are retiring them (A#773).
