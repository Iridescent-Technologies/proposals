The problem

Training that never reaches the job.

Traditional learning teaches people to remember and understand — then hopes it sticks. It rarely does. The knowledge stays in the course, the behaviour never changes on the job, and L&D is left unable to show what any of it was worth.

The gap

Learning that doesn't transfer

Skills practised in the abstract don't survive contact with real work. The transfer gap is where most training budget quietly disappears.

The drift

Generic, and out of date

One-size content ignores the role, the market and the standard — and is stale before it ships.

The silence

No proof for the board

Completion rates aren't impact. When the CFO asks what changed, most L&D has nothing to point to.

Why it's efficient

Two gains, at the same time.

Gain one

The evidence

Not a hunch — decades of research.

Every turn of the flywheel rests on established learning science. The findings below are the ones Zavmo is built to operationalise.

The 2 Sigma benchmark

One-to-one tutoring with mastery learning moved the average student above 98% of a conventionally-taught class — the north star this whole model chases.

Bloom (1984), Educational Researcher. doi
Active learning

Across 225 controlled studies, active learning raised exam performance by ~0.47 SD and made students roughly 1.5× less likely to fail than lecturing.

Freeman et al. (2014), PNAS. doi
Retrieval practice

Actively recalling material — being tested on it — produces markedly better long-term retention than simply re-reading it.

Roediger & Karpicke (2006), Psychological Science. doi
Spaced practice

A synthesis of 317 experiments: spreading learning across time dramatically outperforms cramming it into one session.

Cepeda et al. (2006), Psychological Bulletin. doi
Transfer to the job

Learning only creates value when it transfers to work — and transfer depends on practising in job-like conditions, not just acquiring knowledge.

Baldwin & Ford (1988), Personnel Psychology. doi
Flow

People learn and perform best in flow — the state reached when challenge is matched to skill. It's exactly what Agent 13 tunes for.

Csikszentmihalyi (1990), Flow.
