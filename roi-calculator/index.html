How the model works

Two value drivers — the same two gains, in pounds.

This mirrors the learning efficiency flywheel: the same two gains, quantified on your numbers.

1 · Training time saved

Learners spend fewer hours reaching the same competence because personalised coaching skips what they already know. Valued at their time: hours saved × fully-costed hourly rate.

2 · Faster to competence

People become fully productive sooner. Valued as the salary-value recovered across the weeks of ramp-up you no longer pay for: weeks saved × weekly cost × value-captured.

Deliberately conservative

It leaves out engagement, retention, compliance-risk reduction and content-production savings — all real, all harder to pin to a single number. The figure is a floor, not a ceiling.

Where the defaults come from

The starting values for each lever are informed by the published learning-science evidence — Bloom's 2 Sigma, the active-learning meta-analyses, and the transfer-of-training literature — set deliberately toward the conservative end. See the sources on the Why Zavmo deck. As Zavmo pilots produce real outcome data, we replace these defaults with measured results.
