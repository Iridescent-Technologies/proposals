UM6P × Zavmo — Pilot Delivery Plan

A three-phase pilot across all three streams — Preparation, Live Learning, and Review & Report — followed by a decision window ahead of Phase 2 rollout. 60 learners, three domains, one shared goal: begin rollout by Month 6.

Timing is indicative and flexes to Global Hubs US and UM6P schedules. Weeks shown are relative to kick-off, not fixed calendar dates.
